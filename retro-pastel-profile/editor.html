     quick template guide
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    
          
    
            
    
              
            
    
            
    
              
    1. Click Anything to Edit
    
              
    Click on any text, profile card, link box, schedule, or top domain bar in the preview to edit its content in the left inspector.
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    
              
            
    
            
    
              
    2. Add & Duplicate Sections
    
              
    Hover over any section to duplicate it (or click the duplicate icon), or click + add section at the bottom of any column to insert new widgets.
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    
              
            
    
            
    
              
    3. Reorder in Page Structure
    
              
    Switch to the Page Structure tab in the sidebar to see an overview of all columns and reorder items with the up/down arrows.
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    
              
            
    
            
    
              
    4. Export config.js
    
              
    When you're happy with your design, click export config to download or copy your code and replace config.js in your GitHub repo!
